# Contributing to Tuck

Thanks for taking the time to help.

Bug reports and pull requests are welcome. Please search existing issues first, describe bugs with clear reproduction steps, and keep pull requests focused. If a change affects the interface, include a short explanation and screenshots when useful.

Feature requests may be considered, but there is no guarantee they will be implemented. Tuck is a small personal open-source project: support and response times are best-effort, product direction remains with the maintainer, and contributions may be declined when they do not fit that direction.

Before submitting a contribution:

- Build and test the change on a supported Apple Silicon Mac when practical.
- Do not commit secrets, credentials, signing material, generated build artifacts, clipboard contents, or other personal data.
- Preserve upstream Maccy copyright and MIT license notices.
- By contributing, you agree that your contribution is provided under the repository's [MIT License](LICENSE).
